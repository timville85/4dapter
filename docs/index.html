4dapter Firmware Flasher

Flash official 4dapter firmware over USB — no Arduino IDE required.

Your browser doesn't support this. This flasher needs the Web Serial API, available in Chrome or Edge on desktop. You can still flash firmware the old way — see the firmware README.

1 Choose your firmware

2 Connect your 4dapter

If your 4dapter currently shows up as a serial/COM port, click below and we'll reset it into programming mode automatically.

This firmware doesn't support auto-reset. Press and release the small reset button on the 4dapter PCB now, then continue.

3 Select the bootloader device

Your browser will ask you to pick a device again — the board now shows up differently because it's in bootloader mode. Look for a second Arduino-related entry in the list.

4 Flash

5 Done

Once flashing finishes above, your 4dapter is running the new firmware. Unplug and replug it if it doesn't show up right away.
